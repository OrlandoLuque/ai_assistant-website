ai_virtual_camVirtual camera with 19 built-in video effects

Group
Media
Binary path
src/bin/ai_virtual_cam.rs
required-features
video-io

Purpose

Virtual camera output with 19 built-in video effects (blur, edge detect, sepia, cartoon, chromatic aberration, …). Registers a virtual camera device the OS can expose to any capture client (Teams, Zoom, OBS, browsers).

Build

cargo build --release --bin ai_virtual_cam --features video-io

Usage

ai_virtual_cam --effect blur
ai_virtual_cam --effect edge-detect --source 0
ai_virtual_cam --list-effects
