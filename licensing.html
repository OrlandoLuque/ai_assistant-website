How a commercial license works

ai_assistant is a single-author project. Commercial licenses are negotiated case by case — terms and price depend on your use, scale, and which capabilities you need. There is no self-service checkout and no SaaS sign-up: you reach out, we scope it together, and you get a license that fits.

To make the first email useful, it helps to mention: what you're building, roughly how it will be deployed, and which parts of the library you expect to rely on (for example LLM orchestration, RAG, multi-agent, security, or the distributed runtime).

Frequently asked

Is it really free for personal and academic use?

Yes. PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 grants broad rights for any non-commercial purpose at no cost, including modifying and self-hosting. You only need a commercial license when the purpose of your use is commercial.

What counts as "commercial"?

Broadly, any use intended for commercial advantage or monetary compensation — selling a product built on it, running it inside a for-profit company's operations, or offering it as a hosted service. If you're unsure, email and describe your case; we'll tell you straight.

How much does a commercial license cost?

It's negotiated per case — there's no fixed price list yet. Cost depends on your use, scale, and the capabilities you rely on. Reach out with a short description and you'll get a concrete answer.

Can I evaluate it commercially before licensing?

Evaluation and proofs of concept are fine under the noncommercial license. Moving an evaluation into commercial production is the point where a commercial license is needed — talk to us before that step.

Do you accept outside contributions?

Not yet. This is a single-author project; a Contributor License Agreement (CLA) will be required before any third-party code is accepted.
